Selected Work

Live · Client website & AI assistant

An AI assistant that answers from the consultant’s own work

A consulting practice had years of writing, talks and course material that visitors never found. We designed and built a custom website around it, with an assistant that answers only from that library, and a private drafting desk that turns the same material into first drafts. When a question falls outside the material, the assistant says so and offers a conversation instead of guessing.

  • 265 documents indexed
  • Retrieval on our own servers
  • Drafting desk for the consultant
  • Hand-built theme, no page builder

Prototype · Knowledge graph

Turning a reference book into something you can explore

Instead of a chatbot over a PDF, we are turning a published jazz guide and open music data into a graph of artists, albums, musicians and labels. Questions like “which albums feature Philly Joe Jones?” become walks across real connections. The graph is built; search comes next.

  • 251K connections in the graph
  • Built partly by supervised AI agents

Pilot · Generative media

Short videos in an expert’s own voice, from their own material

A pilot from May 2026: a short script drafted from the expert’s own material, narrated by a voice model trained on their recordings, animated into a talking-head video with synced captions. Roughly one clip in three was good enough to use, and a person reviewed every one.

  • Fine-tuned voice model
  • 7 of 20 clips judged usable
