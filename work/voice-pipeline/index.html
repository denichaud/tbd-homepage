Pilot · Generative media · May 2026

Short videos in an expert’s own voice, from their own material

An expert with deep material and no time to film. In May 2026 we built a pipeline that drafted a short script from the expert’s own body of work, voiced it with a model trained on their recordings, animated a speaking portrait and added word-by-word captions. Roughly one clip in three was good enough to use, and a person reviewed every one.

The pipeline

The voice model has since been retrained. The newer version has not yet been rendered to video.

Decisions that mattered

  • Check the machine’s work automatically

    Voice models sometimes add, drop or slur words. Transcribing every take and scoring it against the script catches this before a person ever listens, so review time goes into judgment, not proofreading.

  • Start with the data

    The voice is only as good as its training clips. Recordings were transcribed and cut into 3–25 second segments before training.

  • Be honest about cost

    A rendered clip took about 80 minutes of GPU time on our own hardware. That is fine for a weekly batch and wrong for anything on demand. Knowing this up front shaped what we recommended.

  • Same voice, different face

    The same audio can drive a photorealistic portrait or an illustrated character. The client can choose how literal the “digital twin” should be.
